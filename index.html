Most practice websites don't break. They just slowly leak patients.

Your site probably looks fine when you check it on your office computer. But a new patient searching on their phone at 9pm may see something different.

⏳

Slow pages

If your site takes a long time to load on a phone, people often hit "back" and choose the next practice on the list.

☎

Hard to call or book

A phone number that isn't tappable, or a booking link buried three clicks deep, adds friction at the moment someone is ready to act.

🔍

Missing Google basics

Missing page titles, descriptions, or business details make it harder for Google to show your practice for local searches.

None of this is obvious from the front desk. A health report finds it in one go and tells you, in priority order, what to fix first.

What the report checks

An automated review of your public pages, translated into plain English.

⚡

Speed

Server response time and page weight.

🔒

Security

HTTPS, SSL certificate, and security headers.

📊

SEO basics

Page title, meta description, H1 heading, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical tag.

📱

Mobile

Whether your site is set up to display properly on phones (viewport).

♿

Accessibility basics

Image alt text and form labels. A starting point, not a legal audit.

📅

Patient actions

Tap-to-call phone link, online booking link, contact form, outdated copyright year.

📍

Local SEO

Dentist / LocalBusiness schema, visible address, and an embedded map.

🔗

Broken links

Links on your site that lead to error pages.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to my website?

No. We only look at public pages, the same ones any patient can see. No logins, passwords, or plugins needed.

Will this slow down or affect my website?

No. The check makes a small number of polite requests, spaced out, similar to a few visitors browsing your site.

Who is the report written for?

For you or your office manager. Each issue explains, in plain English, why it matters to patients, plus a specific fix your web designer or IT provider can follow.

Does this make my site ADA or HIPAA compliant?

No. We check accessibility basics like image alt text and form labels, which is a useful starting point, but it is not a legal audit and not legal advice. For compliance questions, please talk to a qualified professional.

Will fixing these issues get me more patients?

We can't promise specific results, because many things affect how patients find and choose a practice. What we can do is show you clear, fixable problems that make your site harder to find, slower to use, or harder to book from.

What if I'm not happy with the report?

Our guarantee: if your full report doesn't find at least 3 actionable improvements, you get a full refund. Just email us within 14 days of purchase.

Can you fix the problems for me?

Yes. Our Fix-It Package starts at $497 and is quoted after we've seen your report, so you know the price before any work begins. You're also welcome to give the report to your own web person.

How does Monthly Monitoring work?

For $29/month (or $290/year, which is 2 months free) we re-run the full check every month and email you a fresh report showing how your score changed. It's useful after site updates, plugin changes, or a new web vendor. Cancel anytime.

What is the founding-clinic bonus?

The first 25 practices that buy a Full Report also get their first month of Monitoring free. About four weeks after your report, we re-check your site and email you an updated report showing your score change. No card is needed and nothing renews automatically.

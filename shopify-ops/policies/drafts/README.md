## What I need from you

**Business details** (contact info + terms)
- Store name and the registered person or company
- Commercial registration or tax ID, if you have one
- Full address, phone, LINE ID, business hours
- Which email to show. The store's contact email is currently your personal Gmail.
- Are you VAT-registered? If not, I'll remove "including VAT".

**Returns** (suggested defaults in brackets)
- Return window: [14] days
- Report damage within: [3] days
- Non-returnable items: [sale, gift cards, custom-made, opened personal care]
- Change-of-mind return shipping: [customer pays]
- Exchanges: [once per order, size or colour]
- Refund within: [7] business days after approval

**Shipping operations**
- Processing time [1–2 business days] and same-day cutoff [2 pm]
- Delivery times: [1–3 days] Bangkok area, [2–5 days] provinces
- Courier(s), and whether tracking goes by email or SMS
- Cash on delivery: yes or no

## Things to check
- **Return window:** Thailand's direct-sales and direct-marketing rules may give online buyers 7 days to cancel after delivery. Don't set the window below 7 days, and check the current rules with the consumer protection office (สคบ.).
- **Terms, clause 12** makes the Thai version the one that applies if the two languages conflict.
- These are plain-language drafts, not legal advice.

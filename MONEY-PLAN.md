# Fastest ways to turn the report engine into cash

These are ranked by how quickly they can bring in money. All of them use `audit/enamel_audit.py`, which already exists.
Your part: post the listing, reply to buyers, run one command, and send the PDF.

---

## 1. Sell on Fiverr and Upwork (first money in days, no cold outreach)

People already search these sites for "website audit". You don't have to find buyers; they come to you.

**Fiverr gig title:** I will audit your local business website and give you a plain-English fix list

**Packages:**
| Basic, $25 | Standard, $60 | Premium, $150 |
|---|---|---|
| Score + top 5 issues with fixes | Full health report (every check), PDF | Full report + 30-min video walkthrough or a written action plan for your developer |
| 1-day delivery | 1-day delivery | 2-day delivery |

**Gig description (paste as is):**
> Is your website quietly losing you customers? I'll run a full health check on your business website and send you a clear, plain-English report your web person can act on right away.
>
> **What I check:** speed, HTTPS and SSL, security headers, Google basics (title, description, headings, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical), mobile setup, accessibility basics (alt text, form labels), tap-to-call and booking links, local SEO (LocalBusiness schema, address, map), and broken links.
>
> **What you get:** a score out of 100, a priority-ordered fix list, and for each issue, why it matters and exactly how to fix it.
>
> No logins or passwords needed. I only look at your public pages.
>
> Ideal for dentists, clinics, salons, restaurants, hotels, law firms, and any local business.

**Delivery:** `python3 audit/enamel_audit.py <their url> --name "<their business>"`, then print the report to PDF from your browser and upload it.

**Upwork:** create a "Project Catalog" listing with the same text, and send short proposals on jobs that mention "website audit" or "SEO audit". Attach one sample report as a PDF.

## 2. White-label reports for web agencies (repeat income)

Web designers and SEO agencies give away audits to win clients, and they hate making them. Sell them reports with their logo, $15–25 each, or a $199/month bundle of 20.
One agency client can bring in more each month than ten dentists.

**Message to send to agencies (LinkedIn, or the contact forms on their websites):**
> Hi [Name], quick one. Do you use website audits to win new clients? I produce plain-English website health reports (speed, security, Google basics, mobile, local SEO, broken links, each with a fix) that you can send to prospects under your own brand. 24-hour turnaround, $20 each or 20 for $199/month. Happy to run one free on a prospect of yours so you can see the quality. Want me to?

(If an agency says yes, ask me to add your logo option to the report. It's a small change.)

The tool works for any local business. Run the same outreach for chiropractors, vets, physio clinics, med spas, law firms and plumbers. These have the same problems and the same budgets.
Your landing page footer already says "dental practices and local businesses".

## 4. Walk in locally (Pattaya)

Hotels, clinics, dental tourism practices and restaurants in Pattaya live off Google and foreign visitors.
Run a snapshot on 20 of them, print the worst five, and walk in with the printout and a 1,500–3,000 THB price.
In-person sales close much faster than email.

1. Reply to any Fiverr/Upwork messages and the snapshot requests in your inbox.

Make sure the first 5 buyers are happy before you scale up, and ask each one for a review.
